This is a behavioral audit, not a file-count audit. `PASS` requires a concrete implementation plus deterministic tests that exercise the relevant wire contract; constants, default URLs, router registration, and a generic fallback by themselves are not enough. `IN_PROGRESS` means a usable subset exists but provider-specific conversion, streaming, usage, billing, identifier compatibility, or test coverage is incomplete. There are no `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL` rows: deterministic mock-server tests are sufficient for this matrix, so missing provider behavior is locally actionable rather than credential-blocked. No credentialed live upstream-provider calls were run; `PASS` does not claim account validity, regional availability, or current vendor uptime beyond the tested wire/accounting contract.

Channel-type rows also grade the persisted integer identifier. TokenRouter now preserves the reference's deliberate gaps at IDs 28-30 and 32 and matches the post-gap IDs through `ChannelTypeDummy=61`; `TestChannelTypePersistedCatalogMatchesReferenceIdentifiers` is the executable guard. API-type and adapter rows accept an independently designed direct-dispatch equivalent; they do not require TokenRouter to copy the reference's internal API-type layer. Endpoint-type rows grade the reference's advertised `supported_endpoint_types` vocabulary and defaults, not route presence alone; TokenRouter now derives deterministic capability metadata only from authorized, enabled, implemented channels and exposes it through model and pricing catalogs.
